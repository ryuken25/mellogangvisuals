About

Who we are, and how a booking runs.

MellogangVisuals is a photo and video team based in Denpasar. Most of our work is weddings and events around Bali, plus corporate and destination projects when the brief fits.

After you send a date

We check the calendar before we talk about anything else.

You send a date and a location. We check it against the calendar and reply with whether it is open, how many of us would come, and a price range for that kind of day.

If it works, a deposit holds the date. A week before the shoot we send you the running order we will be working from, so you can correct it.

Delivery

What you get, and when.

Turnaround

Photos are ready in three weeks and the film in six. If you need a short teaser sooner than that, say so before the shoot and we will plan the edit around it.

How the files reach you

Everything goes into a Google Drive folder that stays yours: full resolution photos, the graded film, and the camera files if you want them.

Revisions

One round of changes on the film is included. Send us the timecodes and we work through them in a single pass.
