About us

What we do, and how we do it

MellogangVisuals is based in Denpasar. We shoot weddings, prewedding sessions, events, corporate video and destination promos. The crew is small, and we add people when the event is big.

What we cover

The work we usually take

Most of it is weddings and events in Bali. The rest is corporate video, company profiles, and hotel or destination promos. We take work outside Bali too; travel is quoted separately.

If your date clashes with another booking, we say so at the start. We do not hold a date without confirming it.

The process

From brief to delivered files

Pre-productionWe agree the scope, the references and the timeline. If the location allows it, we survey it before the day.
Shoot dayThe crew arrives knowing the run-down. We work around the event, not the other way round.
Post-productionSelection, editing and colour. The final files go out on the agreed date, via Google Drive.
