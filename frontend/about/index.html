MellogangVisuals is a Bali-based photo and video production team working across weddings, events, destinations, corporate projects and brand content.

How we work

Professional from brief to delivery.

We start by understanding the purpose of the project, then plan the right crew, schedule and visual approach. On set, we keep the process organised and collaborative. In post, we focus on a clean finish that serves the brief.

Our work is built around clear communication, reliable production and images that remain useful after the launch, event or celebration is over.
